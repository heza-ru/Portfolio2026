## Learned User Preferences
- Prefers article writing to sound polished, less redundant, and technically substantive rather than basic.
- Prefers the logs/blog UI to feel like a standard publication experience similar to established tech magazines.
- Expects article images to render correctly in both the main logs listing and full article view, with clean placement.
- Wants a straightforward workflow to add new articles and related images directly within the `logs` content structure.

## Learned Workspace Facts
- Long-form article source content is stored in `logs/articles/<id>/article.md`.
- The current active content workflow centers on the `logs/articles` directory for article updates.
